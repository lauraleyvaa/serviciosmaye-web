FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

Do you own trucks in the UAE?

We coordinate a network of vetted, subcontracted hauliers and manage every job end to end. You deal with one person, not a list of drivers.

Which ports and destinations do you cover?

Every UAE port: Jebel Ali, Port Rashid, Khalifa Port, Mina Zayed, Khor Fakkan, Port Khalid, Hamriyah, Ajman, Saqr, UAQ and Fujairah, plus warehouses and free zones. We deliver to all seven emirates.

What do you need from me to quote?

The pick-up, the destination and the cargo (container size or load type, plus weight if it is heavy), and a phone number or email to send the price to.

How do I know my cargo was delivered?

You get a photo of the delivered load on every trip, sent to your email.
