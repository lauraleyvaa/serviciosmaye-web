About us

A family company that grew by delivering.

Servicios Ma&Ye was born in Cuba in 2014 with one idea: grow the food, move it ourselves and deliver it straight to hotels.

Today we bring that same discipline to every load, and we are expanding to the United Arab Emirates, Panama and the Dominican Republic.
