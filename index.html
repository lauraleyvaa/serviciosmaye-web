Who we are

A family business with one simple idea.

Servicios Ma&Ye was born in Cuba in 2014: grow the food, move it ourselves and deliver it straight to hotels.

More than a decade later, we are a fully integrated agriculture and logistics company trusted by leading international hotel brands.

Now we are taking that know-how to Panama, the Dominican Republic and the United Arab Emirates.
